## 1. Layering recovered to ceiling: 1/15 → 3/15, background → ~0

**Reshape adopted** (`examples/amlworld.motif`): `*3..6` → `*5..8`,
`span <= 4d` → `<= 10d`, gaps `quickly` → `patient`, amounts
`within 10%` → `within 25%`, emit 0.7 → 0.5. Train: the three n≥5
instances catch (106 at 1.0, 122 at 0.86, 163 at 0.61); background
1600-anchor sample scores max exactly 0.0. 3/15 is the ceiling — the
other 12 instances have <5 members. Holdout has no n≥5 RANDOM
instances, so holdout validation is vacuous; the mechanism (background
has no long smooth chains) is what carries it.

**Correction to the Nov 1 mechanism story.** Nov 1 said truth is
"slow and amount-volatile" while "background has smooth fast chains".
Direct measurement inverts this: truth is chained (27/28), gaps p50
8.2h, amount wobble p50 3% / p90 10% — while *unconditional*
background walks are slower (p50 24h) and wildly volatile (|log
ratio| p50 2.9). Both are true via selection: the matcher keeps each
anchor's best of many background candidates, and the best background
chain beats truth's typical one. What actually zeroed truth: (a)
`quickly` reads 8h gaps as ~0.35; (b) `within 10%` reads 8-11%
wobble as ~0 (inst 163's steps score 0.21/0.02/1/1/0/1 — min 0).
Length is what separates: at *5..8 background has no witnesses at
all, so the loosened terms are safe.

## 2. Scatter fragments: rejected, structurally out

The having≥1 variants flood background (6.3% of anchors at emit 0.3
for zero new catches) and are rejected; current scatter stands. The
misses are not scatter-shaped at all: 156/158 are **2-cycles**

## 3. Instance 128: time-disordered 7-cycle, round_trip ceiling 8/9

The one missed HI cycle scores no-witness (not low-score): 7 hops
over the *2..5 max, and its chain order violates time (Sep 7 06:35
follows Sep 7 10:06). No time-ordered motif can match it. Round_trip
8/9 is the ceiling, confirmed.

## 4. `all but K`: shipped (grammar → checks → both evaluators)

New fuzzy-only quantifier: (K+1)-th smallest degree, i.e. `each`
with K outliers forgiven; 1.0 when K covers all bindings (the crisp
correct generalization). Completion-only on paths (non-monotone, so
never pruned — pruned/unpruned parity pinned by test). Footgun,
documented in LANGUAGE.md: K must sit below the minimum defined
bindings (hops−1 on paths); on 2-hop cycles `all but 1` vacates to
1.0, which is why round_trip does not adopt it. This is the
robustness primitive the LI n=11 all-zero motivated; no current
motif adopts it yet (layering catches everything at 25% without
it), it is capability for longer motifs and agent search.
Tests: path + group forgiveness with `each` contrasts, E_CRISP pin.

## 5. Three kills, with numbers

- **Graded `having`: no new power.** A crisp `having` gate plus a
  graded `soft` term already expresses everything it would. Dropped
  at design time, no code written.
- **Pairing motif (+ edge-var refs in path soft): overlap too big,
  engine cost too large.** Truth pair |log ratio| p50 0.75 vs
  background 2.29 looks separable until the overlap: truth p90 3.10
  vs background p50 2.29, and 10%+ of time-close background pairs
  match within 50%. Precision would be ~0. It would also need a new
  planner shape (disjoint 2-pattern is E_PLAN today) plus
  cross-edge refs. BIPARTITE stays 0/17 (proven structural).
- **Per-condition weights (§7 item): no customer.** The only
  candidate was 128, which is structural (§3), not a weak-condition
  case. Deferred; `all but K` covers the robustness need.
