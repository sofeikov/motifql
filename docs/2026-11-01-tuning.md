# Nov 1 — Motif tuning: what calibration can and cannot recover

Follow-up to the Oct 30 RQ1 (§8) honest numbers. Question: with the
engine fixed and the ranking honest, can recalibration — breakpoints,
tolerances, windows, thresholds, fitted on train — recover the motifs
that show nothing? Short answer: three recoveries adopted (round_trip,
scatter_gather, gather_scatter), five principled rejections with
evidence. All fits on train; holdout only validates.

## 1. Method

Per motif: background vs own-pattern-truth score distributions on
train, zero-cause diagnosis (structural vs fuzzy-zero, and which term),
truth shape stats (sizes, spans, sender×receiver grids), then targeted
grids measuring train catches (any-member ≥ emit) against fires/day on
both splits. Group replays are cheap (~10-40s/split on 16 workers), so
grids ran full splits; path questions used targeted anchors and saved
scores. Tiny-n caveat throughout: train sets run 6-17 instances, so
every catch count is ±1-2 instances of noise — adopted changes all
have a mechanism story, never a bare count.

## 2. Adopted

**round_trip: emit 0.6 → 0.5.** Threshold sweep on train: 0.5 catches
8/9 (was 4/9) for 32 fires/day (was 22). The four recovered cycles sit
in [0.5, 0.6); background there is ~10/day. Holdout: 3/3 CYCLE (was
2/3) at 113 alerts (38/day), plus broad cross-pattern.

**scatter_gather: window 48h → 7d, drop the `most m` amount term.**
Truth pools funds across mids (instance sums balance 0.90+, per-mid
pairs mismatch badly), so per-mid matching zeroed real scatters — the
same lesson gather's comment already records. And truth spans 60-96h,
overflowing 48h. Grid result: 0/7 → 4/7 train (every catchable
instance: the 3 missed are two single-mid fragments the having clause
excludes by design, plus malformed inst 184 with no pure source/dest).
Background 1/day → ~20/day. Holdout validation is vacuous (the single
holdout instance has 2 transfers).

**gather_scatter: count-balance `within 2` → `within 4`.** The big
truth instance (99, 4×3) runs a diff-2 imbalance that scored 0.0 at
width 2 despite 1.0×1.0 several terms. Width 4 catches it (5/6 train,
was 4/6) for +33% background (31K → 41K/day); dropping the term
entirely catches nothing more at 5× background, so the pressure stays.
Holdout 1/2 unchanged (the miss is a 2-transfer fragment).

## 3. Rejected with evidence

**fan_out / fan_in: keep `many`.** Flipping to `several` catches
everything (11/11, 10/10) — including five single-transfer
"instances" via pure-background groups — at 250-320K fires/day. That
is not detection. Truth fans are small and slow (1-16 receivers over
60-95h) while background hubs are dense; count terms cannot separate
them. Ceilings accepted: 3/11 (n≥7 instances), 6/10. Holdout fan sets
are 1-2 transfer fragments — structurally outside any fan motif.

**layering_chain: unrecoverable by calibration.** Three independent
walls: (a) 12/15 train instances have ≤2 members (seven are single
transfers) — no *3..6 chain can match them, ever; (b) the *2..5
reshape completes on 59% of background while the two missed long
instances top at 0.327/0.023, below background's best — the fuzzy
terms, not the hops, bind; (c) those terms bind because truth is slow
(hours-long gaps vs `quickly`) and amount-volatile (0.023
consistency), while background has smooth fast chains. Loosening terms
lifts background more than truth. Recovery needs redesign
(volatility-seeking terms? OR-semantics?), not calibration. The one
caught instance (122, n=11) stays caught.

**bipartite_butterfly: unrecoverable — proof.** Across all 49
BIPARTITE instances, no two truth edges share a sender or a receiver:
truth is pure 1-1 matchings, and a 2×2 core requires a sender with ≥2
receivers. No threshold, window, or term can match what is not there.
Members can only score via background cores (2/82 do). Options are a
matching-layer motif (needs set semantics, out of scope) or dropping
the motif. Left as is.

**structuring_fan_in: left as is.** A demo motif with no ground truth;
background never fires (having fails 2/3 of the time, the just-below
term zeroes the rest). Tuning it would be shaping noise.

## 6. LI-Small transfer check (targeted replays only)

Question: do the §2-§4 verdicts transfer to LI-Small, or are they
HI quirks? No full replay (banned as too slow): targeted truth-anchor
replays at the adopted calibrations plus 2K background samples
(`/tmp/li_verdicts.py`). LI is even sparser (23 train / 11 holdout
instances, zero FAN-IN train), so rates are ±huge noise — but every
verdict below is structural, not rate-based.

- **round_trip: transfers.** Train 1/2 (n=3 caught at 0.819; n=11
  all-zero — one bad step zeroes an 11-way min), holdout 1/1 (n=4 at
  0.591).
- **layering: transfers, stronger.** 0/6, every member exactly 0.000 —
  including n=5/n=7 instances. LI train RANDOM has no singletons, so
  this is pure term mismatch, not fragmentation.
- **fan_out: transfers.** Train 4/6 (all n≥3 caught; n=2/n=1 miss —
  same shape as HI). Background saturation identical to HI (2K
  sample: p50 0.0, p90 1.0, p99 1.0). The density inversion is a
  data-family property, not HI-specific. FAN-IN has no LI train
  instances; background sample is sparser than HI (p90 0.0, p99 0.8)
  but same heavy-tail shape.
- **scatter: transfers.** All LI scatter truth is n=2 single-mid
  fragments — structurally out, all miss as predicted.
- **gather: consistent.** Train 1/2 (the n=1 "catch" at exactly 0.5 is
  background lottery, not detection), holdout 0/1 at 0.25/0.375.
- **butterfly: proven on LI too.** No two truth edges share a
  sender/receiver across all LI BIPARTITE instances — same 1-1
  matching generator shape.

## 7. Simulator shapes (why calibration hits walls)

The planted patterns (`HI-Small_Patterns.txt`, "BEGIN LAUNDERING
ATTEMPT - FAN-OUT: Max 16-degree Fan-Out", ...) show the verdicts
are generator mechanics, not sample size:

- **BIPARTITE is planted as 1-1 matchings** (each sender gets one
  receiver), not dense crosses — hence the no-2x2 proofs on both
  datasets. A bigger run of the same simulator yields more 1-1
  pairs, not 2x2 cores.
- **Planted fans are small by parameter** (`Max 3-degree Fan-In`),
  smallest in the Small sets — hence fragment-dominated truth.
- **Planted truth contains a self-loop**: the GATHER-SCATTER example
  has account `0048309 → 0048309`. The simulator does not obey the
  graph hygiene the matcher assumes (cf. the C1 self-loop saga).
- **Nominal amount chaos is currency units, not value.** The CYCLE
  example jumps 58702 → 7332 → 26443 across hops in Yuan/Franc/
  Shekel/..., but see §8: in USD terms the cycle preserves value.

Three-way gap: textbook typology (what the motifs encode) vs the
simulator's planting mechanics (what we measure against) vs real
laundering (unobservable here). Calibration closes only the first
gap. Larger AMLWorld sets would fix coverage problems (structuring
has zero HI-Small instances; scatter legs may grow) but not
structural ones (butterfly matching design, layering value crash,
fan background ≥ truth ordering — larger graphs likely worsen fan
precision, since more background senders means more chance fans).

## 8. FX/USD normalization sanity (correction)

Correction to an interim chat claim: cross-hop amount comparison is
NOT apples-to-oranges — prep derives FX from mixed-currency rows
and normalizes to USD (`scripts/prep_amlworld.py`, `fx_rates.parquet`).
Spot-check: the derived rates are sane (EUR→CHF 1.072 n=423,
USD→BRL 5.65 n=757, Yen→EUR 0.0081 n=164) with p95/p5 ≈ 1.0 —
the simulator uses near-fixed rates, so median-implied FX is
trustworthy.

Consequences:

- CYCLE inst 1 in USD: 8764 / 8014 / 7830 / 8050 / ... — value is
  preserved; the nominal chaos is units only. This is part of why
  round_trip recovers.
- The layering value crash (-69% median leg ratio, §3) is therefore
  real in USD terms, not an FX artifact.
- FAN-OUT inst 0 in USD: 3338 / 10112 / 5321 / 5.7M / 7254 / ... —
  wild even normalized. Non-uniform fan amounts are simulator shape.

## 9. Language gaps vs motif-authorship gaps

Audited against `grammar/motifql.lark` + `docs/LANGUAGE.md`. Several
suspected gaps are already expressible — those are motif rewrites,
not language work. Hackathon stance: fitting simulator quirks is
explicitly in scope.

Needs language/engine work:

1. **Baseline-relative counts** (fans). `count(distinct m)` saturates
   in dense background (p90 = 1.0 both datasets); absolute count
   terms cannot separate truth fans from background hubs. Need
   per-anchor history aggregates ("3× this sender's trailing
   rate"). New aggregate type + history lookup in `groups.py`.
   Biggest precision lever. Medium effort.
2. **Graded `having`** (fragments). `having` is crisp by static check
   (`checks.py`), so fragments die at 0 instead of scoring partial
   credit. Proposal: graded having contributing to the score, or a
   `having soft` clause. Small-medium effort.
3. **Per-condition weights** (already LANGUAGE.md §7). The min
   t-norm lets one bad step zero an 11-hop cycle (LI n=11 all-zero).
   Small-medium effort.
4. **Set-valued variables for bipartite/stack** (already §7). The
   principled butterfly fix; large. Hackathon shortcut is a
   pairing-motif rewrite (below) instead.

Motif-only (no language change needed):

- **Layering decay-tolerant rewrite.** `~=` with wide `%` works
  mechanically (`tol_degree`: degree floor rises with the width),
  so expecting decay instead of +10%/day growth is pure authoring.
- **Butterfly→pairing rewrite.** "Sender with exactly one receiver
  in-set" is expressible via `count(distinct) == 1`.
- **Self-loop policy per motif.** `x != y` exists for exclusion; and
  since planted truth contains self-loops (§7), matching them is a
  legitimate hackathon fit.
- **Structuring as-is.** `just_below(limit)` already exists; the
  motif just needs data containing instances.
